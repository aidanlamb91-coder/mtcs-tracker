MTCS Tracker (Beta)

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

MTCS Tracker is a free beta web app for organising an MTCS competence portfolio. This page explains what happens to your data.

Your data stays on your device

The app is local-first. Your portfolios, evidence files and signatures are stored in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on your device. There is no account, and the developer cannot see this data. There are no analytics, adverts or tracking scripts. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it, so keep backups.

MTCS forms packs

When you download an official forms pack, the app fetches it through a small Cloudflare Worker proxy (mtcs-forms-fetch). It passes the zip from MTCS Online straight through and does not store it or anything about you. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the request.

Feedback

If you send feedback (More → Feedback), the app sends what you type, the feedback type, the screen you were on, the app version, the date and time, basic device information (browser, platform, screen size, installed or not) and a name only if you enter one. It goes through the same Worker to a private GitHub file that only the developer reads. Your IP address is used briefly in memory to limit spam and is not saved. Please don't put personal or client-confidential information in feedback.

Exports and sharing

Files leave your device only when you choose to export, download or share them, and they go only where you send them.

Google Drive and OneDrive backup (optional)

If you turn on cloud backup, you sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own screen. The app asks for:

  • Google: the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it creates.
  • Microsoft: Microsoft Graph Files.ReadWrite, User.Read and offline_access.

Backups are saved in your own cloud account. Sign-in tokens stay in your browser. For Google, the one-time sign-in code and later token renewals pass through the same mtcs-forms-fetch Worker, which holds the app's Google client secret; it stores nothing, and your Google renewal token is kept on your device in an encrypted form only that Worker can unlock. When the app is open and connected it backs up automatically (you can switch this off in Backup & restore). The developer never receives your cloud files or tokens. You can disconnect in the app, or remove access any time at Google account connections or Microsoft app permissions (work or school accounts: My Apps).

MTCS Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to save and restore your backups, is not sold, not used for advertising and not read by people.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated, and the date above will change if it is. Questions: via the Feedback form in the app (More → Feedback).
